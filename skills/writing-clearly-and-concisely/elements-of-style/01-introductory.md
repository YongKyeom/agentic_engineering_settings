## I. Introductory

> Editorial note: Historical English reference adapted from Strunk (1918). Consult for its historical principles, not as a universal current style standard. Follow the text's actual language, audience, publication conventions, and meaning-preservation requirements in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

This handbook summarizes the essentials of plain English style. It focuses on the rules of usage and principles of composition most often broken, offering a compact alternative to exhaustive manuals. Master the guidance here, then look to the best authors for finer points of style.
